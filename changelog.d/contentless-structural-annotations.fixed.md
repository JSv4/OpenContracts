- **Parsers no longer create contentless structural annotations** (`opencontractserver/utils/contentless_annotations.py`, applied in `opencontractserver/pipeline/base/parser.py::BaseParser.save_parsed_data`): PDF text layers yield structural elements whose text is only whitespace (e.g. NBSP) or symbol-font glyphs (private-use bullets). They were unsearchable, could not be meaningfully embedded (the embedder returns no vector for a lone private-use glyph), and kept document readiness from completing. Structural, text-only annotations with no letter or digit in any script are now dropped before import; image annotations and non-structural (imported or user-created) annotations are untouched. Children are re-parented to the nearest kept ancestor and relationships lose dropped endpoints (removed when left without a source or target). The document text layer is unchanged.
